## The design changes: this is a repairable system, not a life test

The sketch assumed the scarce-event route leads to Weibayes. That was wrong, and the
reason matters more than the correction.

There is **one** APU, repaired and returned to service four times. These are recurrent
events on a single repairable unit, not four independent lifetimes. Fitting a Weibull
to the interarrival times assumes renewal to as-good-as-new, which is precisely the
assumption a repaired air leak does not license. The right sequence is: test for trend
first, and only then choose the model.

Interarrival times, from a nominal window of 1 Feb to 31 Aug 2020:

```
start -> failure 1    77 d
      -> failure 2    41 d
      -> failure 3     7 d
      -> failure 4    40 d
      -> censored     47 d   (window ends with the unit running)
```

**Laplace trend statistic U = +0.50.** Well inside +/- 1.96, so the null of a constant
rate cannot be rejected. With four events there is no evidence of deterioration and no
evidence of improvement. That is a finding, not a shortfall: it says a homogeneous
Poisson process is the defensible model here, and that anyone fitting a beta to these
four points is reading noise.

Under an HPP with time truncation:

| quantity | point | 90 % interval |
|---|---|---|
| MTBF | 53 d | 23 to 155 d |
| rate | 6.9 /yr | 2.4 to 15.8 /yr |

The interval spans a factor of 6.7. **That range is the deliverable**, not the 53.

Caveat: the arithmetic above uses day resolution and a nominal window start of 1 Feb.
Both need the CSV. The trend conclusion is robust to that (U is far from the critical
value), the intervals are not.

## Item 1, partially closed: the 1 Hz claim is wrong

The repository README said 1 Hz. The UCI page itself says **0.1 Hz**, and arithmetic
settles it: 1,516,948 records over 1 Feb to 1 Aug is 96.5 % of what 0.1 Hz produces,
and 9.6 % of what 1 Hz produces. At 1 Hz the file would have to be 90 % missing.

This entered the repository because the figure was copied from a dataset description
instead of being checked. It is the exact failure the CLAUDE.md rule names. Corrected.

The 15 columns, per the UCI page, still to be confirmed against the file:

```
TP2  TP3  H1  DV_pressure  Reservoirs  Oil_temperature  Motor_current
COMP  DV electric  TOWERS  MPG  LPS  Pressure Switch  Oil Level  Caudal Impulse
```

Note this contradicts the earlier "7 analogue, 8 digital" split, and also the sibling
2022 paper, which describes eight analogue and eight digital signals plus GPS. Counting
the names above gives seven analogue and eight digital, with no GPS. Do not carry the
2022 paper's sensor list into this dataset.

## Do not confuse the three MetroPT datasets

This cost an hour and would have corrupted the life table.

| dataset | period | size | failures | GPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MetroPT-3** (ours, UCI) | Feb to Aug 2020 | 1,516,948 rows, 15 cols | 4, all air leak | no |
| MetroPT (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6854240) | Jan to Jun 2022 | ~10,979,547 points | 3: 2 air leak, 1 oil leak | yes |
| MetroPT-2 | not established | not established | not established | not established |

The 2022 table, read from arXiv 2207.05466, is a different unit-year and must never be
merged with ours:

| # | type | component | start | end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | air leak | air dryer | 28 Feb 2022 21:53 | 1 Mar 2022 02:00 |
| 2 | air leak | clients | 23 Mar 2022 14:54 | 23 Mar 2022 15:24 |
| 3 | oil leak | compressor | 30 May 2022 12:00 | 2 Jun 2022 06:18 |

Several papers in this literature cite "the MetroPT dataset" without saying which. Any
figure taken from a paper has to be attributed to a variant first.
